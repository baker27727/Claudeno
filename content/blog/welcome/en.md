Hi — and welcome to the **Claude Code Learn** blog. This is the spot for
"what's new" notes, deeper dives into specific features, and the occasional
behind-the-scenes look at how this site is built.

Every time a new Claude Code release ships, this blog gets a fresh post
automatically (see the [changelog](/en/changelog) for the source of truth).
A post only goes live after it passes the same automated checks as every
other change on this site — content parity, link validity, and a full
build. There's no manual review step holding it back, and no unverified
content reaches the live site either. That means:

- You can subscribe here for release commentary, not just raw changelog lines.
- Every post is written and checked before publishing — if something can't
  be verified, it doesn't ship.
- English and Norwegian are two separate pages, not one page with both
  languages stacked together — switch with the language toggle, and you'll
  land on the matching post in the other language.

Browse the archive on the [blog index](/en/blog) or jump straight into the
[changelog](/en/changelog) for the raw release notes.
