## Overview

Social media managers face constant pressure to maintain consistent, engaging content across multiple platforms. Claude Code can streamline your entire strategy workflow—from researching trending topics and competitor analysis to generating content ideas and building organized content calendars.

## Why Use Claude Code for Social Media Strategy

**Time savings**: Claude Code can analyze competitor accounts, identify trending hashtags, and suggest content themes in minutes instead of hours of manual research.

**Consistency**: Generate structured content calendars that maintain brand voice across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter).

**Data-driven decisions**: Process analytics data and audience insights to recommend optimal posting times and content types.

**Batch creation**: Use Claude to draft multiple posts, captions, and variations in a single workflow.

## Getting Started: Content Calendar Generation

### 1. Research Your Audience & Trends

Use Claude Code to:
- Analyze competitor social media strategies
- Identify trending topics in your industry
- Extract audience demographics and preferences from your existing data

**Example workflow:**
```
1. Provide Claude with competitor account data or industry reports
2. Ask Claude to identify content gaps and trending themes
3. Get recommendations for content pillars (e.g., "education," "behind-the-scenes," "promotions")
```

### 2. Generate Content Ideas

Provide Claude with:
- Your brand voice guidelines
- Target audience profile
- Key themes or campaigns

Claude can generate:
- 30-day content calendars
- Post ideas for each platform
- Hashtag suggestions
- Optimal posting times

### 3. Create Variations & Adaptations

Claude Code excels at adapting single content pieces:
- Long-form blog post → Instagram carousel → TikTok script → LinkedIn article
- Product announcement → 5 different platform-specific versions
- Trending sound/format → 10 unique takes on your brand message

## Tips for Success

1. **Be specific about brand voice**: Provide examples of good/bad posts so Claude understands your tone.

2. **Use structured templates**: Ask Claude to output content in consistent formats (JSON, CSV, or markdown tables).

3. **Iterate with feedback**: Share performance data with Claude to refine future suggestions.

4. **Maintain a content library**: Use Claude to organize and tag existing successful posts for reference.

5. **Test platform-specific approaches**: Let Claude create platform-optimized versions rather than posting identical content everywhere.

## Limitations & Best Practices

- **Claude generates drafts, not final posts**: Always review for accuracy, brand alignment, and current context.
- **Trend data has cutoff**: Use Claude for evergreen strategy; supplement with real-time trend tools for breaking news.
- **Human oversight essential**: Especially for communities, sensitive topics, or crisis management.
- **Don't over-automate responses**: Use Claude to draft, but personalize customer interactions.
