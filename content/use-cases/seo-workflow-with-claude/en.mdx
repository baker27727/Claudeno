# SEO Workflow with Claude

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) involves complex, repetitive tasks that benefit from AI assistance. Claude Code can help you automate and accelerate SEO workflows, from technical audits to content optimization and data analysis.

## Why Claude for SEO?

Claude Code reads your codebase, edits files, runs commands, and integrates with your development tools—capabilities that translate directly to SEO work:

- **Content Analysis**: Rapidly analyze and optimize large volumes of web content for readability, keyword placement, and structure.
- **Technical Audits**: Automate checks for common SEO issues like missing meta tags, broken redirects, and indexing problems.
- **Data Processing**: Convert raw Google Search Console or Analytics exports into actionable insights.
- **Batch Operations**: Scale manual SEO tasks across hundreds of pages in minutes.

## Core SEO Tasks You Can Automate

### 1. Meta Tag Optimization

Claude Code can audit and fix meta tags across your site:

```
Analyze all pages in /src for:
- Missing or duplicate title tags
- Meta descriptions under 120 or over 160 characters
- Missing H1 tags
- Generate optimized versions based on page content
```

Provide Claude with your page content, and it will suggest title tags and meta descriptions that include target keywords while respecting character limits—a critical task for large sites.

### 2. Internal Linking Strategy

Identify linking opportunities by having Claude:

- Scan your content for keyword mentions
- Suggest relevant anchor text and target pages
- Flag orphaned pages with no incoming links
- Generate a linking roadmap

### 3. Schema Markup Implementation

Add structured data markup (JSON-LD) to your site:

```
For all product pages in /products:
- Extract product name, price, rating, availability
- Generate schema.org/Product JSON-LD
- Inject into template or individual files
```

This helps search engines understand your content and can improve rich snippets in search results.

### 4. Redirects and Canonicals

When restructuring your site or consolidating duplicate content:

- Audit existing 301 redirects
- Identify pages needing canonical tags
- Generate a redirect map
- Add canonical tags to templates

### 5. Content Gap Analysis

Compare your content against competitors or keyword lists:

```
Given a list of 500 target keywords:
- Check which keywords your site ranks for
- Identify high-intent keywords you're missing
- Suggest content topics to fill gaps
- Estimate traffic potential per topic
```

### 6. robots.txt and Sitemap Generation

Automate crawl optimization:

- Generate robots.txt rules based on your site structure
- Create XML sitemaps for Google and Bing
- Ensure all indexable pages are included
- Exclude low-value pages (admin, duplicate content)

### 7. Search Performance Analysis

Process Google Search Console data:

```
Export your Search Console data and ask Claude to:
- Identify top-performing queries and pages
- Find queries with high impressions but low CTR
- Spot ranking trends over time
- Recommend pages to optimize
```

## Practical Workflow Example

### Scenario: Optimizing 100 Product Pages

1. **Audit Phase**: "Analyze our /products directory and report on SEO health—title tags, meta descriptions, H1 tags, and missing schema markup."

2. **Optimization Phase**: "For each product page, generate:
   - A unique title tag (55–60 characters) including the product name and main benefit
   - A meta description (155–160 characters) with a call-to-action
   - Product schema JSON-LD with name, price, rating, and availability"

3. **Implementation Phase**: Claude Code can then apply these changes across all 100 files automatically.

4. **Validation Phase**: "Check that all changes were applied correctly, no duplicates exist, and schema is valid according to schema.org."

5. **Reporting Phase**: "Generate a CSV summary of old vs. new meta tags and highlight any files that need manual review."

## Integration with SEO Tools

Claude Code can prepare data for your SEO stack:

- **Google Search Console**: Export data, analyze trends, and identify optimization opportunities.
- **Analytics**: Combine traffic data with on-page metrics to prioritize improvements.
- **Rank Tracking**: Parse rank reports and correlate with content changes.
- **Keyword Tools**: Process keyword research CSVs and align them with your site structure.

## Best Practices

1. **Start Small**: Test Claude's SEO suggestions on a few pages before bulk operations.
2. **Verify Quality**: Always review generated content and metadata—SEO quality depends on it.
3. **Document Changes**: Keep a log of all modifications for accountability and rollback if needed.
4. **Monitor Results**: Track metrics before and after to measure the impact of Claude's optimizations.
5. **Combine Human & AI**: Use Claude for analysis and automation, but keep human reviewers for final approval.
6. **Respect Guidelines**: Follow Google's SEO Starter Guide (avoid keyword stuffing, prioritize user experience, ensure mobile-friendliness).

## Key SEO Principles (From Google's SEO Starter Guide)

- **High-quality content** is fundamental; optimize for users first, search engines second.
- **Title and meta tags** should be unique, descriptive, and include target keywords naturally.
- **Mobile optimization** is critical—most searches happen on mobile devices.
- **Site speed** matters; Claude can help audit performance bottlenecks.
- **Structured data** (schema markup) helps search engines understand your content.
- **Backlinks** and authority remain important; Claude can audit link health.

## Getting Started

1. Prepare your site structure and content in accessible formats (HTML, Markdown, JSON, CSV).
2. Define your SEO goals (e.g., improve average ranking position, increase organic traffic).
3. Ask Claude to audit specific areas (technical, content, performance).
4. Review recommendations and refine based on your business priorities.
5. Use Claude Code to implement changes at scale.
6. Monitor results using your analytics platform.

## Common Challenges & Solutions

**Challenge**: Large sites with thousands of pages
**Solution**: Batch operations by section; use Claude to generate templates and apply them systematically.

**Challenge**: Balancing SEO with brand voice
**Solution**: Provide Claude with style guidelines and brand keywords; use its suggestions as a starting point for human refinement.

**Challenge**: Keeping up with SEO best practices
**Solution**: Regularly audit your site using Claude and compare against current Google guidance; treat it as an ongoing process.

By combining Claude's analytical power with SEO best practices, you can scale your optimization efforts and focus your expertise on strategy rather than repetitive tasks.
